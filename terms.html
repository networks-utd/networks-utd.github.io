Terms of use

Colab Drive Access is a personal research utility operated by Sam Chen.

Intended use

The utility is intended for the operator’s own research workflow. It is not a public file-transfer service, a university service, or a public account-registration system. No institutional endorsement is offered.

Google services

Google Drive and Colab retain their own terms, storage limits, compute limits, and account policies. A saved credential does not guarantee permanent access; the operator or Google may revoke authorization.

File transfers and availability

Transfers can fail or be interrupted. Retain original data and check uploaded results. The helper downloads to local runtime files and creates new output files in Drive. No public service or availability commitment is offered.

Privacy and access

The privacy notice explains the utility’s data handling. Grant a notebook access to the reusable credential only when its code is trusted. The operator may stop using the utility and revoke authorization through Google Account controls.
